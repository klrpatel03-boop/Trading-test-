## Options Strategies Ranked by Priority

### 1. Debit Spreads (Primary Strategy) — Best for $1,200 Account

Debit spreads are the **bread and butter** for a small options account. They let you take directional bets with defined risk and lower capital requirements than buying naked options.

**Bull Call Spread (Bullish Swing)**
- Buy an ITM or ATM call
- Sell an OTM call at a higher strike
- Max risk = premium paid (debit)
- Max profit = spread width - debit paid

**Bear Put Spread (Bearish Swing)**
- Buy an ITM or ATM put
- Sell an OTM put at a lower strike
- Max risk = premium paid (debit)
- Max profit = spread width - debit paid

**Why this works with $1,200:**
- Typical debit spread cost: $50-$200 per contract
- Defined risk — you know max loss before entering
- Can run 3-4 spreads simultaneously
- 50-200% return on risk per winning trade

**Setup Criteria:**
1. Stock at a key support/resistance level on the daily chart
2. RSI divergence or oversold/overbought bounce
3. Volume confirmation (above average on the setup day)
4. Use 14-30 DTE (days to expiration) for swing trades
5. Strike selection: buy ATM, sell 1-2 strikes OTM

---

### 2. Long Calls/Puts (High Conviction Plays)

For your highest-conviction setups, buying straight calls or puts gives unlimited upside potential.

**When to use over spreads:**
- Strong catalyst (earnings, FDA approval, macro event)
- Stock breaking out of a multi-week consolidation
- Clear trend with momentum confirmation

**Rules for long options:**
- **Always buy at least 21 DTE** — never buy weeklies (theta decay kills you)
- **Prefer 30-45 DTE** for swing trades — enough time for the move to play out
- **Buy slightly ITM or ATM** — avoid deep OTM lottery tickets
- **Position size: max $100-$150 per trade** (8-12% of account)
- **Take profits at 50-100% gain** — don't get greedy
- **Cut losses at 40-50% of premium** — strict stop

---

### 3. Cash-Secured Puts (Income + Entry Strategy)

Sell puts on stocks you want to own at a lower price. If assigned, you get shares at a discount. If not, you keep the premium.

**Best for:**
- High-quality stocks after a pullback
- Generating income while waiting for entries
- Building small share positions over time

**Rules:**
- Only sell puts on stocks you'd actually want to own
- Sell at a strike 5-10% below current price
- Use 21-45 DTE for optimal theta decay
- **Max 1 CSP at a time** given account size (ties up capital)
- Requires enough cash to cover assignment (so pick lower-priced stocks)

---

### 4. Credit Spreads (Premium Collection)

Sell spreads to collect premium when you expect a stock to stay above (bull put spread) or below (bear call spread) a certain level.

**Bull Put Credit Spread:**
- Sell OTM put, buy further OTM put
- Profit if stock stays above short strike
- Max risk = spread width - credit received

**Rules:**
- Sell at 30-delta or below (70%+ probability of profit)
- Use 21-30 DTE
- Close at 50% of max profit — don't hold to expiration
- Max risk per spread: $60-$100

## Trade Setup Playbook

### Setup 1: Breakout Swing (Bull Call Spread)

**Signal:**
- Stock consolidating in a tight range for 5+ days
- Volume drying up during consolidation
- Price breaks above resistance on above-average volume

**Execution:**
- Buy ATM call, sell call 2-3 strikes higher
- 21-30 DTE
- Risk: cost of spread ($50-$150)
- Target: 80-150% of debit paid
- Stop: close if spread loses 40% of value

### Setup 2: Oversold Bounce (Long Call or Bull Call Spread)

**Signal:**
- RSI below 30 on the daily chart
- Price at a major support level (50-day MA, prior low, VWAP)
- Bullish candlestick reversal pattern (hammer, engulfing, morning star)

**Execution:**
- Buy ATM or slightly ITM call, 30-45 DTE
- Or bull call spread if premium is expensive
- Risk: up to 10% of account
- Target: 50-100% gain on the option
- Stop: close if stock breaks below support

### Setup 3: Momentum Continuation (Long Call)

**Signal:**
- Stock in a strong uptrend (above rising 20-day and 50-day MA)
- Pulls back to the 20-day MA on light volume
- Bounces off the 20-day MA with increasing volume

**Execution:**
- Buy slightly ITM call, 30-45 DTE
- Target: ride the next leg up, trail stop
- Stop: close if stock closes below the 50-day MA

### Setup 4: Earnings Play (Debit Spread or Long Straddle)

**Signal:**
- Stock with historically large earnings moves (5%+)
- IV has not yet fully expanded (enter 5-7 days before earnings)
- Clear directional bias from sector/guidance trends

**Execution:**
- Bull call spread or bear put spread based on directional bias
- Or buy a straddle/strangle if direction is unclear but move is expected
- **Size small**: max $60-$100 risk on earnings plays
- Close the day after earnings — don't hold through IV crush

### Setup 5: Mean Reversion (Cash-Secured Put)

**Signal:**
- High-quality stock drops 10%+ on broad market weakness (not company-specific bad news)
- RSI deeply oversold
- You'd happily own shares at the lower price

**Execution:**
- Sell a CSP at a strike 5-10% below current price
- 30-45 DTE
- If assigned, you own shares at a great price
- If not, you pocket the premium

## Risk Management Rules (CRITICAL)

### Position Sizing

| Rule                              | Limit                      |
|-----------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Max risk per trade                | 10% of account ($120)      |
| Max capital in a single position  | 25% of account ($300)      |
| Max total capital deployed        | 80% of account ($960)      |
| Min cash reserve                  | 20% of account ($240)      |
| Max open positions                | 4 at any time              |
| Max correlated positions          | 2 in the same sector       |

### Stop-Loss Rules

- **Debit spreads**: Close if spread loses 40% of value
- **Long calls/puts**: Close if option loses 50% of premium paid
- **Credit spreads**: Close if spread moves against you to 2x the credit received
- **Time stop**: Close any trade that hasn't moved in your favor after 7 days

### Profit-Taking Rules

- **Debit spreads**: Take profit at 80-150% of debit paid
- **Long calls/puts**: Take partial profits at 50% gain, trail the rest
- **Credit spreads**: Close at 50% of max profit (don't hold to expiration)
- **Never turn a winning trade into a losing trade** — use trailing stops

### Daily Checklist

1. Check open positions and P/L
2. Review stop-loss levels — adjust if needed
3. Scan watchlist for new setups using the screener
4. Check economic calendar for upcoming events
5. Review sector rotation and market breadth
6. Log all trades in the trade journal

## Account Growth Expectations

**Read this honestly.** Most retail options traders lose money over time. You are betting on direction *and* timing, and fees plus bid-ask spreads work against you on every trade. Any plan promising 10%+ per month is selling a fantasy — sustaining that would make you one of the best traders alive (Warren Buffett compounds at ~20% per *year*).

### The real goal for your first 3 months

Forget dollar targets. Success early on is:

1. **Don't lose more than ~20% of the account** while you learn.
2. **Honor every stop** — cut losers at -50% of premium, never ride one to zero.
3. **Log 15-20 trades** so the journal shows *your* real win rate, not a hoped-for one.

### Realistic returns once you have a track record

| Scenario                        | Monthly        | Notes                                      |
|---------------------------------|----------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Genuinely skilled + disciplined | +2-4%          | Hard to sustain; this is a *good* outcome  |
| Careful beginner                | break-even to +1-2% | Expect this while learning            |
| Over-sizing / chasing returns   | negative       | The most common path — avoid it            |

> **Reality check**: A skilled trader at +3%/month roughly doubles the account in about two years — and that already beats most professionals. Compounding is powerful but slow on a small base, so the dollar gains feel tiny at first. The traders who survive accept that and never over-bet to speed it up. Keep losers small, let winners run, and let the account grow at the pace it actually grows.

### Scaling Rules

- **Under $2,000**: Max 1 contract per trade, stick to debit spreads
- **$2,000-$5,000**: Can size up to 2 contracts, add credit spreads
- **$5,000-$10,000**: Full strategy deployment, 2-3 contracts, wider spreads
- **$10,000+**: Can start using more advanced multi-leg strategies
