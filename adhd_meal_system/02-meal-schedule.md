# 02 — The Anchor Schedule + Medication Timing

This is the part that actually beats time-blindness. Read it once and set it up.

The schedule has two layers:
1. **Alarms** — the external trigger (your phone yells at you).
2. **Anchors** — attaching each meal to a routine you *already* do reliably.

Both together = the meal happens even when your sense of time and hunger are offline.

---

## The medication problem (and the strategy)

Stimulants (Adderall/Vyvanse/Concerta/etc.) follow a predictable appetite curve:

```
Appetite
  high │█                                              ██
       │██                                           ████
       │███                                        ██████
  med  │████                                    █████████
       │█████                               ███████████
  low  │██████▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁█████████████
       └──────────────────────────────────────────────────
        wake   meds      MIDDAY DEAD ZONE      evening
              kick in   (appetite gone)       (rebound)
```

Three windows, three different strategies:

### 🌅 Window 1 — Morning (before meds peak): **eat real food, fast**
This is your *best* appetite window of the day. Use it. Eat a protein-forward
breakfast within ~30–60 minutes of waking, ideally **before or right as you take your
meds** — once they kick in, the window closes. Front-load protein here because you may
not get much in midday.

### ☀️ Window 2 — Midday (dead zone): **drink your calories**
You will not be hungry. That's expected — do not wait for hunger, it isn't coming.
Forcing down a big plate of food feels gross and won't happen, so **don't fight it —
switch formats.** A protein shake or smoothie goes down when solid food won't. This is
why "DRINK lunch" is the alarm label. Liquid calories are the hack that keeps midday
from being a 0-calorie hole that wrecks your total intake and triggers the rebound.

### 🌙 Window 3 — Evening (rebound): **feed it on purpose**
As meds wear off, appetite comes roaring back — usually right when executive function
is lowest. **This is the Taco Bell hour.** The rebound is real hunger from a low-intake
day; ignoring it doesn't make it go away, it just decides *where* you eat. So you plan
for it: a real, satisfying, high-protein dinner is ready, plus a planned evening snack.
You feed the rebound with the good food so it doesn't feed itself with the drive-thru.

## Your daily anchor schedule

Fill in your real anchors in the right column. Examples given.

| Alarm (tune to you) | Meal | Anchor it to (a habit you already do) | Format |
|---------------------|------|----------------------------------------|--------|
| ~30 min post-wake | **Breakfast** | _"After I pour my first coffee"_ | Solid, protein-first |
| ~12:30 pm | **Lunch** | _"When I take my lunch break / step away from work"_ | **Liquid** (shake/smoothie) |
| ~3:30 pm | **Snack** | _"After my afternoon [walk / meeting / dog out]"_ | Protein snack |
| ~6:30 pm | **Dinner** | _"After I close the laptop / get home"_ | Real meal, the main event |
| ~8:30 pm (optional) | **Evening snack** | _"When I sit down to watch / wind down"_ | Casein/protein + fiber |

> **Habit-stacking formula:** *"After I [existing habit], I [eat the meal]."*
> The existing habit is the trigger your ADHD brain already obeys. The meal rides on it.

## Alarm setup scripts (copy these labels)

Recurring phone alarms, labeled with the *action* so the alarm makes the decision:

- `🍳 EAT NOW — best appetite window, protein first, before meds peak`
- `🥤 DRINK lunch — you won't be hungry, that's normal, drink it`
- `🥜 Protein snack — head off the 4pm crash`
- `🍽️ Dinner — rebound incoming, feed it on purpose (not the drive-thru)`
- `🌙 Evening snack — optional, protein + fiber, then kitchen closed`

Optional but powerful extras:
- A **"kitchen closed" alarm** ~1.5 hrs before bed to bookend eating and stop late
  grazing.
- A **hydration nudge** every 2–3 hrs (dehydration mimics hunger *and* fatigue, and
  stimulants are dehydrating).

---

## If your schedule is irregular (shift work, no fixed wake time)

Anchor to **events**, not the clock: meal 1 = "within 1 hr of waking, whenever that is";
meal 2 = "4–5 hrs after meal 1"; etc. The *spacing* matters more than the exact times.
The tool's `today` command doesn't care what time it is — it just tells you the meals.

---

## Why this beats a normal meal plan

A normal plan says "eat lunch at noon" and trusts you to remember and want to. This
plan says: *an alarm fires → it's attached to a habit you already do → it tells you the
exact food → the food is already in your kitchen → and if you're not hungry you drink
it instead.* Every single failure point of ADHD eating has a backstop.
