## THE ONE RULE
**Eat on the alarm, even with zero appetite.** No hunger cue is coming — it's the meds.
Skipping kills the surplus. No streak to break: miss one → eat at the next alarm.

## DAILY ALARMS (copy these labels to your phone)
- `08:00  🍳 EAT — reheat leftovers / oats / shake. No hunger needed.`
- `12:30  🥡 LUNCH — leftovers (or drink a shake). Eat anyway.`
- `15:30  🥜 Snack — grab protein.`
- `18:30  🍳 DINNER — cook the batch (the fun part). Make extra.`
- `20:30  🌙 Snack — then kitchen closed.`

## THE ENGINE
- Cook ONE big batch (4–6 servings) when the spark hits.
- Leftovers = tomorrow's breakfast + lunch, free.
- Summon cooking energy once; coast the rest.
- Portion into single containers the second it's done.

## GAIN ON NO APPETITE
- Drink calories — a shake goes down when food won't (~560 cal).
- +Olive oil (+120), +peanut butter (+190), +nuts (+170), whole milk, extra rice/oats.
- Protein first, every meal (~30–40g). Eat by alarm, not hunger.

## QUICK MEALS
- **Breakfast:** reheat batch · overnight oats · mass-gainer shake.
- **Lunch:** leftovers (default) · tuna+bean salad · shake.

## TOO FRIED TO COOK (depleted day)
- Open a shake. You ate. Win.
- 90-sec bowl: rice pouch + canned beans + tuna/chicken + cheese + oil (~$1.20).
- PB on a banana + a glass of whole milk.
- Ordering out? Biggest rice + double-protein + beans bowl.

## COOK FROM TESTED RECIPES
- **Budget Bytes** (budgetbytes.com) — every recipe priced per serving. Start here.
- BBC Good Food, EatingWell — free, with nutrition info.
- Serious Eats (technique), Minimalist Baker (fewest steps).
- In the app, open any meal → "Cook it from a tested recipe."

## NUMBERS
- Food budget target: ~$8–10/day (~$250/mo). Rock-bottom ~$150/mo.
- Protein: ~0.8–1 g per lb bodyweight. Fiber: ~30–38 g (adequate, not maxed).
- Gaining: maintenance + ~400 cal. Aim +0.25–0.5 lb/week (weigh weekly, track the trend).
- Andover main store: **Market Basket** → Aldi → Costco/BJ's (bulk).

## THE SYSTEM IN ONE LINE
Remove decisions · attach eating to alarms + habits · every meal has a cook-gear and a
no-cook floor · batch-cook to coast on cheap leftovers · lead with cheap protein + fiber
· plan for the depleted day · never start over.
