THE ONE RULE: Eat on the alarm, even with zero appetite. No hunger cue is coming — it's the meds. Skipping kills the surplus. There is no streak to break; miss one, just eat at the next alarm.

⏰ Daily alarms

8:00🍳 EAT — reheat leftovers / oats / shake
12:30🥡 LUNCH — leftovers (or drink a shake)
15:30🥜 Snack — grab protein
18:30🍳 DINNER — cook the batch, make extra
20:30🌙 Snack — then kitchen closed

🔥 The engine

  • Cook ONE big batch (4–6 servings) when the spark hits.
  • Leftovers = tomorrow's breakfast + lunch, free.
  • Summon cooking energy once; coast the rest.
  • Leftovers are the cheapest AND easiest food you own.
  • Portion into single containers the second it's done.

💪 Gain on no appetite

  • Drink calories — a shake goes down when food won't.
  • +Olive oil (+120), +peanut butter (+190), +nuts (+170).
  • Whole milk in shakes/oats; extra rice/oats.
  • Protein first, every meal (~30–40g each).
  • Eat by alarm, not hunger.
