# 11 — A Worked Sample Week

The clearest way to "get" the system is to watch it run for a week. Here's a full,
realistic 7 days — including a low-energy day and an off-plan moment — showing how the
**cook-once / coast-on-leftovers** engine actually plays out, what it costs, and where
the tested recipes plug in.

Assumptions: lean weight gain, appetite-killing meds, cooks on good days. Times are
anchors, not rules — eat on the alarm.

---

## Before the week: one shop (~$55–65)

From `04-grocery-list.md` / the app's Cart. The big-ticket cheap staples: dried lentils
& beans, 2 dozen eggs, oats, rice, a whole chicken + thighs, canned tuna, peanut butter,
olive oil, frozen veg, bananas, cabbage/carrots/onions, cheese, tortillas, milk, protein
powder, spices. That's most of two weeks of food.

> Main store: **Market Basket**. Buy dried beans, not canned, where you can.

## The week, totalled

- **You cooked 4 times** (Sun big, Tue, Thu, Sat) — and ate ~21 meals.
- **One low-energy day** and **one off-plan night** — both absorbed, no restart.
- **Food cost ≈ $31–36 for groceries** that week (plus the one night out) — well inside
  the ~$250/month target, with leftovers carrying into next week.
- **Protein landed ~140–160g/day**, calories in a gentle surplus, fiber high — because
  the cheap staples (beans, lentils, eggs, oats) *are* the high-protein, high-fiber foods.

---

## The pattern to copy

1. **One big cook on a high-energy day** seeds the week.
2. **Coast on leftovers** for the next 1–2 days — cheapest and lowest-effort food at once.
3. **Quick cooks** mid-week when you've got 20 minutes.
4. **Floor meals** on empty-tank days. Never below "ate protein."
5. **Off-plan? Resume at the next alarm.** No streak, no restart, ever.

Open the app's **Prep planner** to auto-build this for *your* week and generate the
shopping list, or **Decide** when you just can't choose. The tested recipes for every
dinner are one tap away on each meal card.
