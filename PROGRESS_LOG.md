# Progress Log

The instrument. Fill this in — it's how I *know* the work is happening.
See `CONDITIONING_PROGRESS_GUIDE.md` for the why. Copy the weekly block below and
add a new one each week. Keep it to 30 seconds a day: just mark ✅ / ⬜ / ❌.

**Legend:** ✅ done · ⬜ not scheduled today · ❌ missed/skipped · 🚩 red flag (pain, hand tear, sleep <6h)

---

## How to use it
- **Daily (30 sec):** mark the leading indicators for today.
- **Weekly (5 min):** fill the weekly summary line.
- **Every 4 weeks:** fill a Retest block. Week 13 = full retest.
